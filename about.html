About Aura Cleaners

Clean Spaces. Healthy Environments. Better Living.

Company Introduction


Aura Cleaners is a professional cleaning and facility management company committed to delivering reliable, hygienic, and high-quality cleaning solutions for residential, commercial, industrial, institutional, and public spaces.


We combine trained manpower, modern cleaning equipment, professional cleaning chemicals, standardized processes, and quality supervision to create cleaner, healthier, and more productive environments.


We believe professional cleaning is more than removing dirt—it is about maintaining hygiene, protecting assets, improving workplace productivity, and creating spaces people can feel proud of.

Our Vision

To become one of the most trusted and professionally managed cleaning and facility management companies in India, recognized for quality, technology, reliability, and customer satisfaction.

Our USP

“From Routine Cleaning to Complete Facility Care.”

Instead of offering only manpower, we are a professional facility-care partner providing:

Manpower + Machinery + Chemicals + SOPs + Supervision + Quality Control

Our Mission

  • Deliver consistent and professional cleaning services.
  • Maintain the highest standards of hygiene and cleanliness.
  • Use modern machinery and scientifically designed cleaning methods.
  • Provide trained and disciplined manpower.
  • Customize solutions according to each client’s requirements.
  • Build long-term relationships through reliability and transparency.
  • Continuously improve our processes and technology.
